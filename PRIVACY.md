# Privacy Policy — Maps Language Override

_Last updated: 2026-11-02_

Maps Language Override does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data.

- The only thing the extension saves is the language code you choose (for example `ja`). It is kept in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), so Chrome may sync it between your own browsers through your Chrome profile. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.
- The extension does not read the content of web pages, your history, your Google account or your location.
- It only adds the `hl=<language>` parameter to Google Maps addresses using Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API, inside your browser.
- It contains no analytics, no ads and no remote code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ngoohebi/OverrideGoogleMapsAccountLanguage/issues
